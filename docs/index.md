## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Dungeon Escape** ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. **Dungeon Escape is designed to operate completely offline on the user's mobile device.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- **No Personal Information**: We do not require account creation, registration, email addresses, names, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Location Data**: We do not access, track, or collect GPS or device location.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry**: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, crash-reporting SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools.
- **No Advertising SDKs**: The application contains zero advertising SDKs and does not collect advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA).
- **No External Network Communication**: Dungeon Escape does not transmit any gameplay or user data over the internet.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage

All game progress, settings, and scores are stored exclusively on your device via standard Android local storage mechanisms (`SharedPreferences`):

- **High Scores & Level Progress**: Best score, highest level reached, and highest coins collected.
- **Audio & Haptic Preferences**: Music volume, sound effects volume, and vibration toggle preferences.
- **Tutorial State**: Whether the initial tutorial has been completed.
- **Saved Game Sessions**: Active level and player state saved when exiting a run.

This data is never sent to external servers. You can erase this data at any time by clearing the application's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

---

## 4. Permissions

Dungeon Escape requires **no sensitive Android permissions**. The application operates with zero dangerous permission requests (no camera, no contacts, no microphone, no storage, no network).

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Dungeon Escape does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is family-friendly and safe for all audiences.
